My Latest Project

UXReport

is a self-optimizing assistant that analyzes websites and provides automated improvements in UX

However, after signing up, many users feel confused when they land on the dashboard due to a lack of clear onboarding. They don’t know what to do first, can’t identify the next steps, and don’t feel a sense of progress, which prevents them from starting the optimization process.

Increasing engagement and conversions rate

I conducted a Usability Expert Review to evaluate the initial version of UXReport. This method originates from the field of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) and is based on the heuristic evaluation approach introduced by Jakob Nielsen and Rolf Molich.

After the Redesign

Additional information

This project was created as part of my UX Design Implementation final exam, where I received an Excellent grade. UXReport was initially developed as a live product, but was later abandoned due to low engagement and conversion rates. This case study focuses on improving usability, user flow, and creating a more engaging and conversion-focused experience.
